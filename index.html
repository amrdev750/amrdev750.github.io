03Journey

The long, strange
road through medicine.

  1. Year 1 · done

    The building blocks

    Organic chemistry and anatomy — the molecular and structural alphabet of the body. Long nights, colour-coded notes, and the first taste of how much there is to know.

  2. Year 2 · now

    Systems & signals

    Physiology, pharmacology, and microbiology: how the body works, how drugs act, and the organisms that share the stage. The pre-clinical picture is starting to click.

  3. Years 3–5 ahead

    How things break

    Pathology, ENT (ear, nose & throat), and biochemistry — mechanisms, cascades, and the logic of disease. Learning to follow a symptom back to its source.

  4. Year 6 ahead

    The human part

    Bedside rotations, patient contact, and the steep learning curve of talking to real people. The textbooks suddenly have faces — I'm looking forward to that part.

  5. Alongside now

    Building & sharing

    Software, research, and small tools that make medicine a little more human. Still deciding on a specialty — writing code and asking better questions in the meantime.
